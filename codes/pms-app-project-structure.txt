				services (folder)
					service-contract.ts (interface)
					product.service.ts (service class)
				
				components (folder)

					product-container (folder)

routing:
-----------------
url			component
--------------------------------------

http://localhost:4200/edit?id=1
/products/add		AddProduct [to be created in products\components folder]


/home			Home [to be created in shared\components folder]
